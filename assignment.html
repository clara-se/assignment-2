How It Works

The Core Technology

Instead of relying on a continuous supply of electricity, the system uses a special wax-like substance called a Phase Change Material (PCM) contained within a removable heating pad (the WarmPak).

  • Melting and Storing Heat: The WarmPak is placed into an electric heater for about 30 minutes until the material completely melts. If electricity is unavailable, the pad can also be melted using boiling water.
  • Locking the Temperature: Once melted, the material naturally stabilizes and maintains a precise temperature of 37°C (98.6°F)—the exact core body temperature a newborn needs.

The Baby Wrap

Once heated, the WarmPak is slotted into the pocket of the Embrace Nest or Care pouch.

  • Gradual Release: The pouch is made of medical-grade, biocompatible fabrics that safely regulate the transfer of heat from the pad to the infant.
  • Longevity: It keeps the baby safely warm for 4 to 8 hours on a single charge, completely independent of the power grid.
  • Reusability: The entire system can be sanitized and reused hundreds of times.

Supporting Kangaroo Mother Care

Traditional incubators require constant power and structurally isolate the baby from the parents. The Embrace warmer is designed to be highly portable, allowing parents to hold, feed, and bond with their baby while maintaining a safe body temperature.

The Embrace infant warmer has helped over 1.4 million babies survive and stay warm.
